How to build this

Cover. Prepare. Reveal.

  1. Cover: keep the current page mounted and fade one fixed overlay over it.
  2. Prepare: behind the overlay, render the next route and await only its above-the-fold data, fonts, and decoded images.
  3. Reveal: fade out the heart completely, then uncover the painted destination while it settles into place.

Readiness controls the reveal. A timer only sets the minimum visual beat.

After reveal, load the rest. Always support reduced motion, focus the new page, and provide delayed status and recovery.
