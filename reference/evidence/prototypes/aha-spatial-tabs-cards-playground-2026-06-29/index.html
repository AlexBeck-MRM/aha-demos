Adaptive image colour

AA · 4.5:1
Colour override

The automatic colour is the default. Overrides are darkened if needed to meet the same contrast target.

How it works

Sample the visible crop near the text. Keep its colour, then find the lightest shade that passes the selected contrast target.

Contrast is checked across the image and fade behind every line of white text. If the fade cannot pass, the text moves onto a solid colour below the image.

AA is 4.5:1; AAA is 7:1 for normal text. These checks cover text contrast, not every accessibility requirement.
